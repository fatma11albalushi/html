Welcome to the Portal

This online system allows registered taxpayers to file returns, manage payments, and track submissions online. The portal is designed to simplify communication between taxpayers and the tax authority.

Use the navigation menu above to explore filing options, review your payment history, or contact support if you need assistance. New users must verify their email before submitting a first return.

Filing types include VAT, CIT, and PIT.

Announcement: VAT Filing Deadline Extended

The deadline for quarterly VAT filing has been extended by two weeks to accommodate the recent system maintenance window.

All pending VAT submissions will now be accepted until the end of the month without penalty.
